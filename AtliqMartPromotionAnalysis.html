COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS OF ATLIQ MART'S PROMOTIONAL CAMPAIGNS

Project Overview

This project represents a detailed analysis of Atliq Mart’s promotional campaigns during the Diwali 2023 and Sankranti 2024 seasons. Utilizing advanced data analytics and visualization techniques, I dissected the performance of various promotion types across multiple store locations in Southern India. The project’s findings have significant implications for future marketing strategies and operational efficiency.

Project Introduction

Atliq Mart, a leading retail chain in Southern India, conducted extensive promotional campaigns during two major festive seasons—Diwali 2023 and Sankranti 2024. As the primary analyst for this project, my role was to evaluate the effectiveness of these campaigns by analyzing large datasets using SQL and Power BI. The goal was to identify trends, assess the impact of different promotion types, and provide actionable recommendations to enhance future campaigns.

Detailed Process Steps

1. Data Collection and Preparation

This stage involved the meticulous extraction and cleaning of data from Atliq Mart’s sales and promotional databases. Using SQL, I aggregated data from multiple sources, ensuring consistency and accuracy across the entire dataset.

  • Data Sources: Sales transactions, promotional events, product details, and store information.
  • Challenges: Handling missing values, normalizing data formats, and ensuring data integrity.
  • Tools Used: SQL Workbench for data querying and manipulation.

2. Integration of SQL and Power BI

After data preparation, I integrated the cleaned data into Power BI. This integration allowed for dynamic and interactive data visualization, enabling real-time insights into sales performance across various dimensions.

  • Setup: Establishing connections between SQL databases and Power BI for seamless data flow.
  • Data Transformation: Applying Power Query transformations to refine and optimize data for visualization.
  • Output: A comprehensive Power BI dashboard with interactive filters and drill-down capabilities.

3. Creation of KPIs and Dashboards

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) were developed to measure the success of promotional campaigns. These KPIs were visualized in the dashboard to provide clear, actionable insights.

  • Key KPIs: Incremental Revenue (IR), Incremental Sold Units (ISU), Average Order Value (AOV).
  • Dashboard Features: Customizable views, comparative analysis, and trend visualization over time.
  • Value Addition: Enabled stakeholders to make data-driven decisions based on real-time analytics.

4. Analysis of Promotion Types and Store Performance

This critical analysis involved evaluating the performance of different promotional strategies, such as cashback offers and BOGOF (Buy One Get One Free) promotions, across various store locations.

  • Promotion Analysis: Identified the most and least effective promotions in terms of revenue and sales volume.
  • Store Performance: Analyzed store-specific data to pinpoint top-performing and underperforming locations.
  • Outcome: Insights into regional market dynamics and customer behavior, guiding targeted promotional efforts.

Key Findings

  • BOGOF and Cashback Offers Lead in Revenue and Sales: BOGOF and cashback promotions generated the highest revenue and sales, outperforming discount-based offers.
  • Personal Care Products Lagging: Personal care products had lower sales, likely due to the focus on other categories during the festival season.
  • Home Appliances and Home Care Excellence: The Home Appliances and Home Care categories performed exceptionally well, contributing to 50% of overall sales and revenue post-promotion.
  • Major Revenue from Key Cities: 50% of the revenue was generated from stores in Bengaluru, Chennai, and Mysuru, highlighting the significance of these markets.
  • Stores Expansion: Hyderabad though with higher number of stores lagged in Incremental Revenue / Incremental Sold Units during promotions.

Strategic Recommendations

  • Focus on High-Performing Categories and Products: Prioritize promotions for top-selling items like Home Appliances and key products.
  • Prioritize Effective Promotion Types: Emphasize cashback and BOGOF offers, and reassess underperforming percentage-based discounts.
  • Optimize Regional Strategies: Strengthen campaigns in Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad; tailor promotions for underperforming regions and try to establish more stores in Madurai and Vijayawada.
  • Enhance Customer Engagement: Create customer-centric promotions with immediate benefits and implement loyalty programs.
  • Leverage Data Analytics: Continuously monitor and analyze promotional performance; use predictive analytics for optimization.
